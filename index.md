## About me

I'm a sophomore at Cornell University majoring in Biomedical Engineering. I enjoy finding creative solutions to everything from problem sets to rigging broken dorm furniture. I was born and raised in Colorado, and am an adventurous hiker, skier, and mountain biker. 

I'm an undergraduate researcher in the Feschotte Lab at Cornell, and am working with Drosophila to learn about Transposable Elements and their importance in reproduction and development. I have a strong interest in molecular/cellular solutions to complex biomedical problems and would like to get more hands-on experience to broaden my understanding of related fields. I'm currently looking to do so through an internship or Co-op program.
